AussiePrep Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

AussiePrep is an independent Australian citizenship test preparation application. We respect your privacy and aim to collect as little information as possible.

Information We Collect

AussiePrep does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the app.

Study progress, answers, statistics and app preferences are stored locally on your device.

Analytics and Advertising

AussiePrep does not currently use advertising or third-party analytics services to track your activity.

Location and Device Permissions

AussiePrep does not use your device location, contacts, camera or microphone for its core functionality.

How Your Data Is Stored

Information used to maintain your study progress is stored locally on your device. We do not sell or share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

Deleting Your Local Data

You can remove locally stored app data by clearing the app's data through your device settings or by uninstalling AussiePrep.

Children

AussiePrep is a general educational application. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the way AussiePrep handles information changes, this Privacy Policy may be updated. The updated version will show a new "Last updated" date.
